## English

**App name:** Aria: Voice Reminders (21)

**Subtitle (App Store):** Your caring personal assistant (30)

**Short description (Play):**
Say it once, Aria reminds you — by voice or notification. Bills, medicines, family. (79)

**Full description:**

Aria is a personal assistant you talk to. Say "remind me to take my BP
tablet at 9 every morning" or "add milk and eggs to the grocery list".
Aria sets it up and reminds you on time, in a voice you choose or as a
quiet notification.

Made for busy people and their parents:
• Reminders by voice or tap, in English, Hindi or Hinglish
• Rings on time, even offline, after a restart, or when you travel
• Medicines, bills (with amounts), birthdays and "call Dad" nudges
• Morning briefing and night wrap-up
• Simple Mode with big text and only what matters, for elders
• Lists and notes you can ask for later ("what's the wifi password?")
• Location reminders: "buy milk when I leave work"
• Family Circle: share lists and remind Mum and Dad from your phone; see
  when it's done
• WhatsApp reminders (Family plan): reply DONE to tick them off
• Works with your phone calendar

Private by design: your voice is turned into text on your phone and audio
is never stored. You can export or delete everything at any time.

Free forever for everyday reminders. Personal, Family and Team plans add
every voice and theme, routines, location reminders, shared Circles and
more. 7-day free trial, cancel any time.
